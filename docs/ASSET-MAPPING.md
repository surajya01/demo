## 1. Overview & Policy

Per Phase 1 architectural directives:
- **Genuine Photographs Only:** We use strictly genuine, verified resort photographs extracted from the official property documentation and archives.
- **No Stock Imagery:** We do NOT download random stock photos or fabricate artificial resort scenes.
- **Controlled Selection:** Rather than cluttering the production project with hundreds of duplicate resolutions or uncurated files, we have selected only high-fidelity, verified assets.
- **Clear Placeholders:** All pending, unverified, or missing room/menu images are mapped to clearly branded SVG placeholders.
